Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Applying

How do I apply?

Open the role you're interested in and select "Apply now". The form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number and profile link, and a short message about why the role suits you.

Can I upload my CV?

Not at the moment. Instead, add a link to your CV, portfolio, GitHub or LinkedIn profile in the application form.

Where is the work based?

Our office is in Yendada, Visakhapatnam. Each role lists its own location and work arrangement where these have been set.

How is my application information used?

Only to consider you for the role you applied for. It is visible only to authorised KK Digital Growth staff. To have your application deleted, email admin@kkdigitalgrowth.in.
