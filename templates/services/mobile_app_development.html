Critical Mobile Challenges Solved

We eliminate common mobile development pitfalls that cause negative app ratings and abandonment:

  • App Store Rejections: We conduct rigorous pre-flight checks against Apple App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play Developer Policies to ensure approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Mobile App Development Questions

Objective answers regarding cross-platform tech, store publishing, and ongoing OS compatibility.

Why do you recommend Flutter over building separate native apps?

Flutter compiles down directly to native ARM machine code for both iOS and Android, allowing near-identical native performance, fluid 60fps animations, and native device feature access from a single shared codebase. This cuts development timelines by approximately 35-40% and drastically lowers long-term maintenance costs compared to maintaining two distinct codebases.

How do you handle App Store and Google Play publishing?

We manage the submission process end-to-end: generating distribution certificates, provisioning profiles, preparing store assets, and adhering to Apple App Store and Google Play developer guidelines. We guide your team through developer account ownership so all apps are published under your company's official corporate developer account.

Can the app function when users lose internet connectivity?

Yes. Depending on your business requirements, we design local SQLite / Hive caching layers that store essential data locally, allowing users to browse records offline and automatically queue updates to sync once a connection is restored.

What happens when Apple or Google release new OS versions?

Both iOS and Android release major updates annually. We offer ongoing maintenance agreements that test and update your app's dependencies, ensuring continuous compatibility with new operating system releases, screen dimensions, and privacy permissions.
