Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Digital Marketing

Can you guarantee first-page rankings or a place in AI answers?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Search engines and AI tools decide independently what to show. We focus on the things that make a site easier to understand and more useful, and report honestly on what changes.

How soon will we see results?

Paid ads can bring visits as soon as campaigns go live. Organic search changes take longer, because search engines recrawl and re-evaluate pages on their own schedule, so we don't promise a timeframe.

Should we start with SEO or ads?

If the website has technical problems, fixing them first helps both, because ads send visitors to the same pages. We'll recommend an order after reviewing your site and accounts.
