Who Our ERP Systems Are Built For

Our custom ERP development services serve organizations with demanding operational workflows:

  • Industrial & Discrete Manufacturers: Facilities needing nested Bill of Materials (BOM) tracking, work order routing, shop floor logging, and material consumption reconciliations.
  • Aerospace & Defense Contractors: Companies requiring component serializations, AS9100 / ISO compliance audit trails, and strict Engineering Change Orders (ECO).
  • Multi-Warehouse Distributors: Businesses managing stock across regional facilities needing batch tracking, reorder alerts, and automated vendor RFQ matrices.
  • High-Compliance Enterprises: Organizations that must retain complete control over sensitive manufacturing records, proprietary CAD drawings, and cost accounting data.

Critical ERP Bottlenecks Solved

We eliminate operational disconnects that create production delays and financial blindspots:

  • Fragmented Departmental Spreadsheets: Replace disconnected Excel sheets across procurement, stores, and shop floor with an integrated relational database.
  • Inventory Reconciliation Discrepancies: Gain real-time ledger visibility across raw materials, work-in-progress (WIP), and finished goods.
  • Delayed Procurement Approval Cycles: Automate multi-tiered Request for Quote (RFQ) distributions, vendor comparative evaluation, and purchase order signoffs.
  • Compliance & Audit Vulnerabilities: Enforce mandatory quality inspection checklists, non-conformance reports, and material lot traceability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About ERP Development

Clear facts on timelines, legacy data migration, and software maintenance.

How long does a custom ERP development project typically take?

Timelines depend on the number of departments and modules required. A focused multi-warehouse inventory and procurement ERP typically takes 3 to 5 months, while comprehensive multi-department systems encompassing engineering BOMs, finance, and quality compliance range from 6 to 12 months. We deploy modules iteratively so your teams see value early.

How do you migrate existing historical data from legacy software or Excel?

We write automated data extraction and transformation scripts that clean, de-duplicate, and validate historical records (vendors, component catalogs, transaction ledgers) before importing them into the new relational schema during a planned staging cutover.

Can the ERP be hosted on our private cloud or on-premise servers?

Yes. We package the ERP platform using Docker containers, allowing you to deploy either to private cloud instances (AWS, Google Cloud, DigitalOcean) or on-premise company servers behind your internal corporate firewall for strict data sovereignty.

Are there any recurring per-user licensing fees?

No. Unlike off-the-shelf commercial ERP vendors that charge monthly per-user licenses, custom ERP systems developed by KK Digital Growth belong entirely to your company. You can add unlimited employee accounts without licensing fee increases.
