Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Software Projects

Should we use WordPress, Shopify or a custom build?

It depends on what your team needs to edit and what the site has to do. WordPress suits content-led sites your staff update often, Shopify suits product stores, and a custom build suits sites or applications with logins, workflows or integrations that those platforms can't handle cleanly. We'll recommend one after discovery.

Will we own the source code?

Yes. Once the project is completed and payment obligations are met, you receive full ownership of the source code and project assets.

Can you continue a project someone else started?

Yes. We review the existing code first, identify issues, and then continue development from its current stage.
